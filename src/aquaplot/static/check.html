Step 2 · Photos

Two photographs

One of the water, taken from the bank, showing the channel and the margin. One of what lives in it: scoop gravel and leaves from a fast, shallow patch into a pale tray or tub of water, let it settle for a minute, and photograph what moves. Put everything back afterwards.
