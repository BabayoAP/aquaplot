Check a stream

A few short steps at the water's edge. You identify what lives there, a model double-checks you, and you get a health reading for the stream and what it means for the people and animals around it. No account, and it works with or without a signal.

Not at a stream right now?

Try a sample check with two real photos: one of a stretch of stream, and one of a tray holding a single insect larva (a nymph). Name the animal yourself in step 3 (a stonefly, perhaps?) and see what the model says after looking at the tray without your answer. The model's reading of these two photos was recorded once and is replayed, so the sample works even on a server with no model set up.

Step 2 · Photos

Two photographs

One of the water, taken from the bank, showing the channel and the margin. One of what lives in it: scoop gravel and leaves from a fast, shallow patch into a pale tray or tub of water, let it settle for a minute, and photograph what moves. Put everything back afterwards. The tray photo is what lets the model double-check your identifications in the next step. Full instructions and safety.

Step 3 · Animals

What did you find in the tray?

Tap each kind of animal you can see. Go by shape: count the tails, and look for legs, a case or a shell. If you're not sure of the exact kind, pick "some kind of…" for its group. That still counts. The model looks at your tray photo separately, without seeing your answers, and tells you afterwards if it saw something different.
